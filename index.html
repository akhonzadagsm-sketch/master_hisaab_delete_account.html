1. How to Delete Your Master Hisaab Account

Users of Master Hisaab can delete their account and associated data at any time using either of the two official methods below. You do not need to keep the app installed to request account or cloud data deletion.

Method 1 — Instant In-App Deletion

Delete Directly Inside Master Hisaab

  1. Open the Master Hisaab app and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to the Settings (ترتیبات) tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account (اکاؤنٹ حذف کریں).
  4. Review the data summary, check the confirmation box, type DELETE (and enter your Security PIN if enabled), then tap Delete Permanently.

Your Cloud Firestore records and local device records for that account are deleted and verified immediately.

Method 2 — Web / Email Request (No App Required)

Request Deletion Without the App

  1. Use the Deletion Request Generator below on this page, or send an email directly from your registered Google email address to akhonzadagsm@gmail.com.
  2. Include the subject line: Delete My Master Hisaab Account.
  3. Specify whether you want Full Account & Cloud Data Deletion or Partial Data Deletion.

Verified email requests are processed within 7 business days.

3. Verification & Processing Procedure

  • Ownership Verification: To protect shopkeepers from unauthorized third-party deletion of their financial records, we verify that the deletion request originates from the exact Google Account Email Address registered with Master Hisaab.
  • In-App Verification: In-app deletion verifies your active Google Sign-In session (request.auth.uid == userId), requires explicit checkbox and keyword confirmation (DELETE), and checks your Security PIN if configured.
  • Email Verification: When submitting a deletion request via email, send the email directly from your registered Google account address. If sent from a different address, we will send a single verification reply to the registered Google email before purging the account.
  • Processing Timeline: In-app deletions complete immediately once online verification succeeds. Email deletion requests are verified and permanently completed within 7 business days, followed by a final confirmation reply.
